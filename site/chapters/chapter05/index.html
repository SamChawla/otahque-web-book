CHAPTER FIVE

Django’s role and our first architecture decision

We have described Otahque’s needs, followed a web request, and practised the Python underneath the application. We can now ask what Django contributes to that application and why we would choose it. Understanding that choice will help us explain the files we create in Chapter 6.

By the end of the chapter, you will be able to:

  • distinguish framework capabilities from Otahque’s product rules;
  • follow a request through Django’s main components;
  • sketch the browser, application, and storage boundaries;
  • compare an architecture choice against requirements and trade-offs;
  • record a decision with its reasons, consequences, and status.

You will produce a responsibility map, a small proposed architecture sketch, and a draft architecture decision record. Chapters 1–4 supply the requirements, web vocabulary, and Python readiness. This chapter explains the design; project creation begins in Chapter 6. This draft currently contains Section 5.1 of the five planned sections.
