Complete six-part pre-release for review. Public pages are unchanged; the rule of one active registration per member and event arrives in Chapter 21.

Working towards: First release (Chapter 34). One community runs free events. Members sign up, register, see, and cancel their registrations; organizers publish their own events; two people cannot both take the last place. Tests check each product rule. All milestones

CHAPTER TWENTY

Relationships between models: registrations and organizers

Stage 3: Data and the database (Chapters 15 to 23)

Prerequisites: Chapters 4, 7, and 15 to 19, including the Chapter 19 project, its 30 passing tests, its three events, and the local superuser from Chapter 18

What you’ll build: A new registrations app and two connected models, Registration and OrganizerAssignment, whose foreign keys refuse to delete history, explored in the Django shell and proved by tests written first.
